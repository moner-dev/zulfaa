2. About ZULFAA

ZULFAA is an Islamic app that brings together prayer times, Qibla direction, the Qur'an, tafsir, adhkar, du'a, Islamic occasions, reminders, and a daily knowledge challenge. It is designed and developed by MONER INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS for Android and iPhone.

You should be legally able to accept these Terms where you live; if you are a minor, use ZULFAA with the consent of a parent or guardian. You are responsible for your own device and for the settings you choose — such as your location and calculation method — because what the app shows you depends on them.

4. Religious and informational content

ZULFAA offers Islamic tools and content to support your daily worship. It does not issue religious rulings and is not a substitute for qualified scholars or your local religious authority. For matters that require a ruling or a considered opinion, please consult people of knowledge.

5. Prayer times and qibla

Prayer times are calculated from the settings you choose — such as calculation method, madhhab and any manual adjustments — and from the location in use. Qibla direction depends on your location and on your device's sensors, and its accuracy can be affected by magnetic interference, device limitations, location accuracy or system timing. For anything important, we recommend confirming times and direction with a trusted local source.

6. The Hijri calendar and Ramadan dates

The Hijri dates the app shows — including upcoming occasions and the beginning and end of Ramadan — are calculated from the Umm al-Qura calendar, a published arithmetic projection. The real beginning of a month is settled by moon sighting or by the authority you follow, and can differ from the calculation by a day in many countries in many years. What the app shows is therefore an estimate, it says so, and it is not on its own a basis for a religious decision. Choosing which authority to follow is yours alone, and where they differ, theirs is the answer.

7. Confirming the start of Ramadan yourself

Where the Ramadan Planner lets you confirm the date the month began for you, that confirmation is what the app uses to label the days of the month, in place of the calculated estimate. It is yours alone: it is not published, not suggested to anyone else, and it is not a religious announcement or a substitute for asking the authority you follow. Changing or withdrawing it re-labels the days and nothing more — it never alters, moves or deletes anything you have recorded. The end of the month remains an estimate either way, because confirming when a month began does not confirm when it ends.

8. Umrah Savings

Umrah Savings is a personal planning tool: you type a goal, a currency, and the amounts you say you have set aside. ZULFAA is not a bank, a financial institution, a payment provider or an investment service. It holds no payment details, connects to no bank account, moves no money, holds nothing on your behalf, and gives no financial advice. The figures you enter are not proof of a balance and are not a commitment by anyone to you; they are your own record of money you keep somewhere else. ZULFAA does not sell, book or arrange Umrah travel.

9. Qur'an, tafsir, adhkar and their sources

The Qur'an text, translations, tafsir, adhkar and du'as come from established external sources, each credited inside the app. ZULFAA does not claim ownership of that content; it remains with its rights holders and subject to their terms. Content fetched from external services may change or become unavailable for reasons outside our control.

Some renderings are ZULFAA's own rather than a published edition: the English and Dutch du'a titles; the Dutch renderings of the meaning of Qur'anic du'as; the Dutch of the prophetic du'as and of the adhkar, which includes several Qur'anic passages rendered into Dutch; the English and Dutch of the Daily Challenge questions; and, in two adhkar, an English rendering made by ZULFAA where the compilation's own English does not match the Arabic shown with it. These are credited to ZULFAA inside the app, are offered as an aid to understanding rather than as a translation of the Qur'an, and have not been reviewed by an independent scholarly body. Where precision matters, rely on a published edition and on people of knowledge.

10. Notifications and reminders

When a reminder actually arrives can depend on your device, its operating system and its battery settings, so precise delivery cannot be guaranteed on every device. Prayers set to adhan mode play the adhan bundled with the app where that is supported.

The Daily Challenge is an in-app knowledge feature, and tokens are an internal ZULFAA score that reflects your activity in it. Tokens are not money and not cryptocurrency. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and cannot be redeemed for money or any financial benefit. Honor Board rankings may change over time. Where answers are automated, manipulated, fraudulent or otherwise abused, we may correct or remove scores, or restrict access, to the extent that is proportionate.
