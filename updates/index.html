Qur'an

Your place in the Qur'an is kept

Your place in a surah is kept whether or not recitation is playing, a finished surah steps aside, and the Qur'an tile says which of its two things it will do. The full review of the Qur'an experience is still under way.

  1. Reading counts as readingA position you could return to was only ever recorded while recitation audio was playing, so someone who reads silently never had one and was never offered Continue reading.
  2. The Qur'an tile says which of its two things it will doWith a saved reading position it reads Continue, in the accent colour; with none it stays the plain way in.
  3. A finished surah stops offering to continueReading or playing a surah to its last ayah used to leave every Continue surface pointing at that last ayah, which is not a place to return to.

  1. Improved
    The Qur'an tile says which of its two things it will do

    With a saved reading position it reads Continue, in the accent colour; with none it stays the plain way in. It always behaved differently in those two cases — now it tells you which.

  2. Fixed
    Reading counts as reading

    A position you could return to was only ever recorded while recitation audio was playing, so someone who reads silently never had one and was never offered Continue reading. Scrolling through a surah now records where you actually are, once the page settles — on the start page, on the Qur'an tab and in the reader itself.

  3. Fixed
    A finished surah stops offering to continue

    Reading or playing a surah to its last ayah used to leave every Continue surface pointing at that last ayah, which is not a place to return to. A finished surah now steps aside and the most recent unfinished one is offered instead. Recitation audio is unchanged: it still plays, highlights the ayah, follows it, and records progress as before.

Prayer

The Prayer page, put right

Every row now says what it will actually do, the countdown and the highlighted prayer finally agree after Isha, and where a time genuinely cannot be calculated, ZULFAA says so instead of guessing.

  1. Every prayer row shows what it will actually doEach row now carries its own icon for the choice saved against that prayer: a crossed-out bell means silent, a bell means a notification, a horn means the adhan.
  2. Far north, you can choose how the short summer night is handledPrayer settings has a new High-latitude rule — Twilight angle, Middle of the night, or One seventh of the night — for places and seasons where the sun never reaches the Fajr or Isha angle, as in northern Europe in June.
  3. Prayer settings can be used with a screen readerThe ten plus and minus buttons that adjust each prayer by a minute had no spoken name at all — they now say which prayer and which direction, and the value announces itself when it changes.

  1. New
    Far north, you can choose how the short summer night is handled

    Prayer settings has a new High-latitude rule — Twilight angle, Middle of the night, or One seventh of the night — for places and seasons where the sun never reaches the Fajr or Isha angle, as in northern Europe in June. It has no effect anywhere the sun does reach it, and the existing default is unchanged, so nothing moves unless you change it.

  2. Improved
    Every prayer row shows what it will actually do

    Each row now carries its own icon for the choice saved against that prayer: a crossed-out bell means silent, a bell means a notification, a horn means the adhan. A mixed set-up reads at a glance — Fajr on the adhan, Dhuhr on a notification and the rest silent is three different icons rather than three identical bells. The state is spoken in words to a screen reader as well, so it does not depend on recognising a shape or a colour.

  3. Improved
    Prayer settings can be used with a screen reader

    The ten plus and minus buttons that adjust each prayer by a minute had no spoken name at all — they now say which prayer and which direction, and the value announces itself when it changes. The calculation-method list and the Asr madhhab pair report which option is selected instead of showing it only in colour, and the location line says the place you have set and that tapping it changes it.

  4. Improved
    Dhuhr and Asr no longer share a picture

    Both rows carried the same sun. Asr now has its own late-afternoon glyph, so the two midday prayers are told apart by their icon and not only by their place in the list.

  5. Works differently
    The rows always show your own choices

    If Android has notifications turned off for ZULFAA, a single line at the top of the section says so and offers the way there — but no row changes, and nothing you have set is altered. The same line appears if Android is allowed to delay alerts, so you can ask for them to arrive exactly at prayer time. Nothing is ever switched on for you.

  6. Works differently
    Where a prayer time genuinely cannot be worked out, ZULFAA says so

    Beyond the Arctic and Antarctic circles the sun can stay up, or stay down, for weeks. Fajr, Maghrib and Isha are derived from sunset and sunrise, and without them there is no answer to give: those times now show a dash with a short explanation, the countdown moves to the next prayer that can be calculated, and Dhuhr and Asr — which do not depend on the horizon — are shown as usual.

  7. Fixed
    After the last prayer of the day, the page agrees with itself

    Between Isha and midnight the highlighted next prayer showed that morning's Fajr — a time already hours past, and a minute or two away from what the countdown beside it was counting down to. It now shows tomorrow's Fajr, labelled Tomorrow, at exactly the time the countdown is running to. The start page's prayer card was showing the same wrong time and is fixed with it.

  8. Fixed
    Tapping a prayer's icon steps through all three states

    Off, then a notification, then the adhan, then off again. It used to skip one of them, and which one it skipped depended on what a different prayer was set to: if any prayer was on the adhan, every other prayer jumped straight from silent to adhan and the plain notification could not be chosen from this page at all. The Prayer page and the reminder settings have always written the same saved value, so a change made in either shows immediately in the other.

  9. Fixed
    With no location set, the page no longer names a prayer

    It used to show a prayer name and a countdown before it had anywhere to count for. It now says prayer times appear once you set your location, and offers the picker. The line under the heading is punctuated properly in English and Dutch too — it used an Arabic comma in every language, and a stray one when nothing was set.

  10. Fixed
    The progress bar is visible in dark mode

    Its unfilled track had been the same colour as the card behind it, so early in an interval the bar read as a small green dot floating in the card. Light mode is unchanged.

Ramadan

The Ramadan Planner

Suhoor and Iftar from the same times the Prayer screen uses, five acts of worship a day, and a fasting record that only ever says what you told it.

  1. The planner asks whether you fastedIt used to record a day as fasted the moment you ticked anything at all.
View all Ramadan changes (2)Hide the full list
  1. New
    The Ramadan Planner

    The day of Ramadan, Suhoor and Iftar taken from the same prayer times the Prayer screen uses, and a countdown on the app's own clock. Five acts of worship to keep for each day, and a du'a for the day from ZULFAA's own collection. With no location set it says so instead of inventing times.

  2. Works differently
    The planner asks whether you fasted

    It used to record a day as fasted the moment you ticked anything at all. It now asks, records only the answer you give, and lets that answer be withdrawn. A day can be left unanswered, and fasting is not scored — a day you were excused from is not a day you failed.

Umrah

Umrah Savings

A goal, the contributions you record against it, and everything else worked out from those two — never stored, never touching a bank.

  1. A way back, in the goal's own settingsReset the savings and keep the goal, or delete the goal and its record altogether.
View all Umrah changes (2)Hide the full list
  1. New
    Umrah Savings

    Set what you are saving towards, then record what you put aside. ZULFAA works out what is saved, what remains, how far along you are and how many days are left — none of it stored, all of it read back from your own figures. ZULFAA holds no money and connects to no bank.

  2. New
    A way back, in the goal's own settings

    Reset the savings and keep the goal, or delete the goal and its record altogether. Both ask before they act, and the second asks twice.

A quieter app

Panels you can pull back down, nothing hidden under the navigation, a theme switch that is a switch, and a start page that reads the same in all three languages.

  1.   1. Improved
    The start page can be navigated with a screen reader

    The Verse of the Day card now says that it opens the verse in the reader, and names the verse, instead of reading out a long Arabic string and the word button. Quick Access — the page's main way in, eight tiles of it — is a heading, so it can be reached by heading as well.

  2. Improved
    A panel that slides up can be pulled back down

    Every panel that rises from the bottom draws a small handle at its top edge, and that handle now does what it looks like it does: drag it down and the panel follows your finger, let go and it either closes or settles back. It also stays put while you scroll a long list, instead of scrolling away with the content. Tapping outside, the close button and the Back gesture all work exactly as before.

  3. Improved
    A panel sits over the page instead of replacing it

    Opening the city picker used to black the screen out in dark mode and wash it pale in light, so the panel felt like a separate screen. The page behind is now softly frosted and still visibly there, in both themes; and in dark mode the panel is a shade lighter than the page and the cards on it lighter again — depth rather than one dark slab. Every other panel in the app shares the change.

  4. Works differently
    Portrait only, on phones

    The app is laid out for portrait and now says so, instead of rotating into a landscape it was never drawn for — where the floating navigation left about two lines of screen visible. Large screens still rotate.

  5. Works differently
    Light and Dark change in one frame

    The interface used to fade from one theme into the other, and not all at once — some cards finished before others, so you could watch the change sweep across the screen. It now behaves like a light switch. Every other animation in the app is unchanged.

  6. Fixed
    The verse reference reads the right way round

    The citation under the Verse of the Day was rendered right to left in every language, so an English or Dutch reader saw it reversed. Arabic was always correct and is unchanged.

  7. Fixed
    Dutch and English cards read like the others

    A long Dutch label pushed the reward chip onto a line of its own and wrapped the occasion title onto two, so Dutch alone saw a different composition. English had the same trouble in the finished-challenge state. Both now sit as Arabic always did.

  8. Fixed
    Nothing sits under the navigation, and nothing floats above a gap

    Every scrolling screen reserved more room than the floating bar needs — about fifty pixels too much on a phone — so the last card on Prayer, the Qur'an, Qibla, Adhkar, Profile, the start page and every sub-page ended in a visible empty band. Fixed once, for all of them.

  9. Fixed
    The side menu shows its last line

    At the foot of the menu the version line sat behind the Android navigation bar with only its top edge showing. It now clears the system area properly, in every language and both themes.

Worth knowing

How it behaves

ZULFAA does not estimate a prayer time it cannot calculate

Where the sun never reaches the angle a prayer is derived from, the time is shown as unavailable with a short explanation. Nothing is filled in on your behalf.

How it behaves

Nothing is ever switched on for you

If Android is blocking or delaying ZULFAA's reminders, the app tells you and offers the way to Android's own settings. It changes nothing you have set.

How it behaves

On a phone, ZULFAA stays in portrait

The app now declares portrait rather than rotating into a layout it was never drawn for. Tablets and other large screens still rotate.

Still being written

Named here so its absence is visible rather than accidental.

The Qur'an experience is still being reviewed

A full review of reading, recitation, translation and tafsir is under way. Whatever it changes will be added here before this update is released, and nothing about it is written down yet.

The Privacy Policy is revised with this release

Umrah Savings and the Ramadan Planner record things the current policy does not describe, so the policy in the app, the text on this website and the Play data declaration are all updated at the same time as the release — not before it.
